# Phone app — behaviour

How the phone app behaves, list by list, including what happens when the two phones race each other. The rules themselves live in `src/domain/` and are pinned by the tests; this page describes them for a human. The design behind them is [docs/spec.md](spec.md), §6 and §10. Back to the [README](../README.md).

The app is in French. The labels quoted below, once each:

- **Menu** — the meals planned; **Courses** — the shopping list; **Reste** — what is left at home.
- « Retirer » remove · « Annuler » undo · « Réessayer » retry · « Abandonner » give up · « Mettre à jour » update.
- « ajout » an item added by hand to Courses · « On en a déjà » we already have it · « On n'en prend pas » we are not buying it · « manque » short of.
- « Passage en caisse » checkout · « Saisir le total » enter the total · « Pas acheté » not bought · « Acheté sans ticket » bought without a receipt · « Valider » confirm.
- « Cuisiné » cooked · « Fini » finished · « Il en reste » some is left · « Presque fini » nearly finished · « Changer la date » change the date · « Racheter » buy again · « Retirer du menu » drop from the menu · « Cuisinés récemment » recently cooked · « réservé » held for a meal.
- « plat cuisiné » a dish cooked in a first round and eaten in a second · « assaisonnements » seasonings · « Envies » wishes for the next planning.

## Courses

what to buy, sorted by aisle, with estimated prices and a total against the weekly budget. A product two plannings each listed shows on one row; ticking it buys the lines it showed. An item ticked as bought moves into Reste. « Retirer » on a row takes it off the list: an ajout at once, a planned line after a question, « On en a déjà » (its meals first take what Reste already holds of it free, never from an item « presque fini », and only what they still need goes into Reste, reserved for them, not a whole pack; when a single meal still needs some and Reste holds that product with no quantity, not « presque fini » and for no meal, that meal takes it and nothing new goes into Reste; a staple, or a line whose meals are all cooked, goes into Reste with no quantity, to be told later) or « On n'en prend pas » (the meals it served show « manque » on their recipe; a staple comes back at the next planning if it is still low); « Annuler » for 5 s takes the removal back. A tick on a line the other phone took off meanwhile is refused (« retiré des courses »): nothing enters Reste, and if it was bought after all, add it back as an ajout. A second removal of a planned line already taken off (the other phone answered the other way first) is refused too, « déjà retiré des courses », and shown.

## Installing, offline use and sync

Both phones add it to the home screen from the browser (« Ajouter à l'écran d'accueil »); its icons are drawn at build time from `public/icon.svg`. It shows the last lists it received at once, even with no network, and refreshes when opened, when it comes back to the foreground, and when the network returns. A tick shows straight away and stays on the phone until the server confirms it; a tick left without an answer (the network cut in the shop, the server down) is sent again after 2, 5 and 15 seconds, then every 30 while the app is open; one the server could not record is sent again, and after six tries waits for « Réessayer » or « Abandonner ». When the Access login has expired the app says « Reconnexion nécessaire » and keeps the ticks for after « Se reconnecter ». A new version shows « Mettre à jour » and changes nothing until it is tapped.

## Checkout

« Passage en caisse » records where, the receipt total (optional, typed in euros) and the date, today in Paris by default. When the trip recorded last, whatever date it was given, has no total, « Saisir le total » under Courses names its day and place until the total is entered there or with `npm run plan -- total`; an older trip still without its total is completed with the CLI, whose `status` lists them all. A trip recorded without a receipt (lines bought another day or without a ticket, which leave the basket) has no total and never gets one; it is not the « last trip », and neither `status` nor the calibration reads it. The ticket sheet stays on the ticket it opened for: if a newer one arrives meanwhile, it sends nothing. « Passage en caisse » likewise records the basket it opened on: if a refresh changes it meanwhile, it sends nothing. Opening the sheet reads the server first (waiting 2 s at most, so a dead zone does not hold the checkout), then lists the basket's rows under the count, each a checkbox, so a line the other phone ticked before is on this receipt. A row left checked goes on the receipt; one unchecked asks why, and starts on « Acheté sans ticket » (the answer that undoes nothing): « Pas acheté » takes the tick back (the same as unticking it in « Dans le panier », so it is disabled, with « acheté il y a trop longtemps pour être annulé », on a row bought too long ago to be undone; a row left on it whose time ran out while the sheet was open stops « Valider » with the same words, and nothing is sent; a row whose line left the basket meanwhile is disabled without that note, the basket having changed) and « Acheté sans ticket » records it on a trip of its own without a receipt, place « Autre », dated as the receipt (by the date field alone, today by default, when no row is left on it). With no row left checked the place and total are hidden, the date stays while a row is « Acheté sans ticket », and no receipt trip is recorded; a total already typed stays in sight instead, and « Valider » answers « Aucune ligne cochée pour ce ticket » until it is cleared or a row is checked again; « Annuler » after the checkout takes both trips back but does not re-tick the « Pas acheté » rows. It sends the lines it shows, and the server attaches those and no other: a line ticked on the other phone after that read stays in the basket, and if a shown line has left the basket by the time the checkout lands (the other phone recorded it, or took its tick back), nothing is recorded and the phone says « panier modifié depuis ».

## Menu and recipes

A meal on Menu opens its recipe: the ingredients for its portions, « réservé » on what Reste holds for it, one portion's nutrition from Ciqual values, counted on the edible share of the bought weight (« partiel » names the ingredients without any; the assaisonnements, spices in negligible amounts, are left out, the salt is counted), the steps, and « Cuisiné ». A meal a declared leftover left short of a product says « manque : comté 30 g » under its Menu row and on its recipe, where « Ajouter aux courses » covers it (free Reste stock first, then Courses; « Annuler » takes it back); the phone clears the line at once and the server's answer brings the Courses line. The Cuisiné sheet takes a verdict, a note and, for each Reste item the meal holds, « Fini » or « Il en reste » (what is left, and « Presque fini »); all of it is optional. « Fini » is already on, and a tap clears it, for the plat cuisiné a second round eats and for each item the meal uses up, all of it held by this meal alone; that pre-made tick, marked « fini s'il ne reste rien après ce repas », finishes the item only if, when the cook reaches the server, it is left at 0 and no other meal holds it (a receipt corrected, the other phone's « Il en reste » or a new meal on it in the meantime keeps it). Nothing leaves Reste without a tap. « Fini » on an item another meal also holds asks first whether to take that meal off the menu or to buy it again. A second round waits until its first round is cooked. When both phones cook the same meal, the server keeps the first sheet; the other phone says so if its own sheet held a verdict, a note or a « Fini » the cook tapped (a pre-made one alone stays quiet), which can then be redone from Reste. « Cuisinés récemment » lists the week's cooked meals: a tap on one opens its recipe read-only (the same recipe, nutrition and portions, without « Cuisiné » or the « réservé » marks, even for a meal cooked offline; a Cuisiné sheet left open while the other phone cooked the meal gives way to it, saying what was not sent), and its row's « Annuler » undoes the cook while it is listed; « Retirer du menu » drops a meal. Envies go under the menu, one line each, a web address (http or https only) kept as its link, and one that does not parse left in the text without a link, for the next planning.

## Reste

Each Reste item opens a sheet: « Fini », « Il en reste » (what is left, and « Presque fini ») and « Changer la date »; « Fini » on an item a meal holds asks first, as in the Cuisiné sheet, and « Racheter » puts what that meal needed back on Courses. An item at 0 that no meal holds has « Fini » beside its row, one tap, undone by « Annuler ».

« Il en reste » sets the item's quantity: its unit stands beside the field, so « 125 » is read as 125 g, and « 1,5 kg » or « 2 boîtes » (with a known pack) are converted. Words that are no quantity (« un fond ») are kept, the quantity then unknown, never a false 0; an empty field keeps the quantity, or makes a 0 unknown. What is declared is the truth even below what other planned meals hold of the item: their reservations shrink to fit, the most recently planned meal first, and a meal left short is listed under « Manques » at the next planning. Something at home that Courses never listed is added at the top of Reste; it has no date until one is set. Anything added with no network reads « en attente » and opens nothing until the server has it.
